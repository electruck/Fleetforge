The role

What does an AI Operations Lead do at FleetForge?

You are the human intelligence on top of our AI engine. Our software generates business cases via LLMs, but every output needs to be accurate, consistent and tailored to customer feedback. You manage that production chain — from prompt design to output validation to continuous improvement.

Concrete responsibilities

  1. 01
    Design and maintain AI workflows

    Build and maintain agent flows in n8n, LangGraph or Make for TCO analyses, cashflow projections, EU subsidy applications and leasing proposals. Iteratively improve based on customer output.

  2. 02
    Develop and version-control prompts

    Write, test and optimize prompts for Claude, GPT-5, Gemini. Document what works and what doesn't. Maintain a prompt library with Git-style version control.

  3. 03
    Guarantee output quality (human-in-the-loop)

    Every generated business case is reviewed by you for consistency, accuracy and customer fit. You are the last human link before a quote reaches a transport CFO.

  4. 04
    Integrate data from external sources

    Connect our AI to Odoo (ERP data), grid operator APIs (capacity per region), VLAIO/AFIF subsidy databases, Milence/Fastned charging tariffs, EU SSTPA registry. MCP, API connections, webhooks.

  5. 05
    Documentation and internal tools

    Maintain a knowledge base on Belgian/Dutch transport regulation, grid operator procedures, EU subsidies. Build internal tools that speed up the work of the founder and yourself.

  6. 06
    Evaluate models and tools

    The AI stack changes weekly. Test new models (Claude Opus 4.7, GPT-5, Gemini 2.5, open-source alternatives), new tools (Cursor, Replit Agent, MCP servers). Decide what we adopt.

What equity in a seed startup means

Equity is potentially large upside with substantial risk. At a successful Series A in 2027 (€3-7M at FleetForge €15-30M valuation), 1% equity would be worth €150-300k on paper. At an eventual exit (5-7 years later), this can be multiples — or zero if the company fails. We're honest: 70% of seed startups don't reach Series A. But if you join early and the company works, you get real financial upside.

How we work

What does a typical week look like?

Culture

Small team in seed stage means: lots of freedom, lots of responsibility, fast feedback loops. You have direct access to the founder and all decisions. No meetings to schedule a meeting. We value written communication over verbal (Slack, Notion, Linear) so async work works.

A typical week

  • Monday morning: 30-min sync on weekly goals (everything you plan to do shared async the night before)
  • Through the week: Build/improve AI workflows, review customer cases, test prompts, integrate external APIs
  • 1-2 deep-work blocks: 4-hour uninterrupted sessions for complex work (designing a new agent, building an MCP server)
  • Friday afternoon: "Friday Lab" — free time to discover new AI tools, run experiments, learn about a topic
  • Travel: 2-4 times per quarter to a customer or partner (bank, grid operator). No more.

Working with the founder

The founder has a unique combination of transport logistics (Master + Applied Economics), operational credibility (CE truck license + container experience), logistics IT (Katoen Natie), EV energy (SolarEdge), truck pre-sales (Iveco). He has warm intros at our anchor customer through family. You'll work directly with him, not through layers of managers — which is both an advantage and a disadvantage (intense, no filter).
