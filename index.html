01 · The problem

Everyone wants to electrify. Nothing moves.

The trucks exist. The banks have capital. The EU subsidies are budgeted. Grid operators want flexibility partners. But no single party connects these layers into one bankable deal per fleet. The result: a market frozen by coordination failure, not by technology.

Why now

From August 2026, Dutch and German road tolls rise sharply for diesel trucks while zero-emission trucks stay exempt until 2031. EU AFIR forces infrastructure rollout. The competitive window for early movers is 18-24 months.

Problem 01

Fleet CFOs are stuck

6-18 months per investment decision on TCO analysis, sensitivity scenarios, financing comparison. No time for AI-truck specifics like battery degradation, residual value, charging strategy, grid congestion risk. Decisions are deferred or sub-optimal.

Problem 02

Banks have no deal flow

BNP Paribas Fortis Leasing, Belfius, ING Lease want green transport financing but lack TCO models, origination channels, and internal expertise on e-truck residual value, battery health, or charging infra risk. They watch from the sidelines.

Problem 03

Grid operators are overrun

Fluvius, Stedin, Liander, Elia, TenneT face 2-7 year waitlists for new heavy-duty grid connections. They need predictable flexibility partners — not thousands of individual fleet applications. Currently, that aggregator doesn't exist.
