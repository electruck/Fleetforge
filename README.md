# FleetForge

**AI orchestrator for fleet electrification.** Bankable business cases between fleet operators, banks and grid operators.

🌐 **Live:** [electruck.github.io/Fleetforge](https://electruck.github.io/Fleetforge/)

---

## What we do

FleetForge generates complete bankable business cases for any European transport operator's transition from diesel to electric trucks — in days instead of the 6-18 months it traditionally takes.

We integrate:
- TCO analysis and ROI scenarios across multiple truck models
- Grid congestion data from Fluvius, Stedin, Liander, Elia, TenneT
- EU subsidies (AFIF, CEF-T, AFIR, VLAIO, SPULA/SPRILA)
- Bank leasing options through partner financial institutions
- SSTPA secure parking and MCS charging strategy
- Battery storage and V2G arbitrage opportunities

All into a single deliverable, ready for board approval.

## The five-layer network

1. **Software** — AI business-case engine (Claude, GPT-5, Gemini orchestrated via n8n / LangGraph)
2. **Banks** — Bank-verified deal origination with European leasing arms
3. **Lease revenue** — Recurring revenue share on trucks, chargers, battery parks
4. **Distribution (DSO)** — Flexibility orchestration with Fluvius, Stedin, Liander, ORES, RESA, Enexis
5. **Market (TSO + BRP)** — Aggregator into Elia, TenneT, day-ahead and intraday markets

## Why now

- **August 2026:** Dutch and German road tolls rise sharply for diesel trucks; zero-emission trucks exempt until 2031
- **2030:** EU AFIR mandates infrastructure rollout (3.6 MW charging pool every 60 km on TEN-T core network)
- **2035:** 30% of EU heavy-duty fleet must be electric — 750,000 trucks
- **2-7 year waitlists** for new heavy-duty grid connections in BE/NL
- **€3B+ EU subsidies** allocated but underutilized due to application complexity
